# Atelier 2.11.19 — Sign back in without losing your place

When your Claude sign-in expires mid-session, Atelier now gives you a way back in instead of a dead end.

- **A "Sign in" button when a session signs out.** If Claude's login or token expires while you're working, the composer is replaced by a clear "Your Claude session signed out" strip with a Sign in button — no more cryptic red error with no way forward.
- **Your conversation is preserved.** After you sign back in (in your browser, as always — Atelier never sees your password), the session reconnects with `--resume`, so you pick up exactly where you left off.
- Also fixes the sign-in sheet not always closing itself after a successful login.
